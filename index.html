Assistant Professor of Accounting
Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile

I am an Assistant Professor of Accounting at the Business School of Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile.

I received my PhD in Accounting and Finance from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid in 2024 and was awarded the Extraordinary Doctoral Award.

My research lies at the intersection of accounting and corporate finance, with a particular focus on private firms, financial reporting, and debt markets. I enjoy combining novel research designs, large-scale data, and machine learning to answer economically relevant questions.
